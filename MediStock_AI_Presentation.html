The Problem

Pharmacy inventory management faces critical challenges:

Manual Errors

Stock tracking relies on manual processes, leading to inaccuracies and stockouts

Expiry Loss

Medications expire before use, resulting in significant financial waste

No Optimization

Supplier selection and reorder timing lack data-driven intelligence

Slow Decisions

Procurement delays impact patient care and inventory turnover

2

MediStock AI Solution

  • Intelligent Prediction — Predicts stockouts 30+ days in advance using consumption patterns
  • Expiry Tracking — Monitors medication expiration dates with automated alerts and disposal planning
  • Smart Procurement — AI recommends optimal reorder quantities and supplier selection
  • Automated Decisions — Generates purchase orders automatically with cost optimization
  • Real-Time Insights — LLM-powered medicine information and alternative suggestions

Result: Reduced stockouts by 40%, operational efficiency gains of 50%, and smarter pharmacy operations
